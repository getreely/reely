# Reely

Movie and TV automation for your screening room — monitor what you want to
watch, grab it the moment it hits your indexers, upgrade it until it meets
your quality cutoff, and give every person in the house their own
collection while one file on disk backs every copy.

New here? The [README](https://github.com/getreely/reely#readme) has the
quick tour and the Docker one-liner. The wiki is the deeper reference:
